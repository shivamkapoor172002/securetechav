About

SecureTech AV Designs: Independent. Engineered. Accountable.

Specialists in audio-visual system integration.

Since our incorporation in Noida in August 2022, SecureTech AV Designs has been engineering audio, video, lighting, acoustics and ICT environments for corporate, education, government and hospitality clients — starting with the conference rooms, auditoriums and architectural acoustics that decide whether anyone in the back row can actually hear what's being said.

We don't just supply equipment — we engineer the room around it, from acoustic survey and BOQ through design, tender, installation, commissioning and the maintenance contract that follows. We hold certified OEM relationships and no obligation to any of them — which is why clients trust us to write the specification as well as build to it.
